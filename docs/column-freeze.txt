COLUMN FREEZE MANIFEST for the v2 corpus read (roadmap 12.17)

What this file is
-----------------
The v2 read (12.18) will be measured against a fixed set of per-second
CSV columns. This manifest is where that set is frozen: one line per
field, signed in by the OWNER with a date, and later signed out by the
owner with a date if a field retires. The freeze protects a signed
field from being renamed or removed while a read depends on it — the
columnFreeze guard (tools/columnFreeze.mjs) compares every signed-in
field below against the live CSV_COLUMNS in src/core/csv.ts and goes
red if one is gone. Self-retiring on the drozyGuard pattern: signing a
field out lifts its constraint without deleting its history.

How to sign
-----------
One field per line, inside the signatures block at the end, in exactly this
shape (the guard parses nothing outside that block):

  fieldName | signed in 20 September 2026
  fieldName | signed in 20 September 2026 | signed out 3 October 2026

Only the owner signs. Nothing here was signed by the tool that created
the file: per roadmap 12.17 the manifest and its guard land first, and
the owner-signed freeze is dated after 12.16's and 14.9a's columns
exist and before 12.18a's plan is committed. Until the first sign-in,
nothing is frozen and the guard holds no constraint.
