HOW HARD THE WITHIN-SUBJECT BAR IS (added 7 September 2026, roadmap
10.10c2, ladder B11). The "k/5 agree" column below asks that at least
3 of the 5 multi-session subjects agree on the sign of an effect. Under
the null each subject is a coin flip, so that bar is cleared 0.500 of
the time by nothing at all: 3, 4 or 5 of 5 is (10 + 5 + 1) / 32. A bar
cleared half the time by chance is not a bar, and it alone granted the
three "suggestive and unconfirmed" verdicts below. Read that column as
a description rather than as evidence.

The table itself is NOT regenerated to carry this note. Recomputing it
needs a re-extract from DROZY.zip and would recreate the derived video
DATASETS.md requires destroyed, so the arithmetic is stated here and
recomputed by test from the counts printed below
(analysis/tests/test_analyse_drozy.py). The tool prints the rate beside
each row from its next run onward.

Any bar added to this project after this date must state its chance
rate, and may not grant a verdict on its own unless that rate is below
0.05.
