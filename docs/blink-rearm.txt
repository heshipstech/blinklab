THE RE-ARM GATE: the double-count investigation, predictions first
==================================================================

Written 20 August 2026, after the validation round and before any
experiment ran. The owner chose this investigation over the blunt
alternative (a longer refractory timer) the same day.

THE DEFECT, from the round
--------------------------

P1, a sound-baseline session, produced 25 detections for 10 deliberate
blinks. Eleven pairs of detections sit closer than 0.35 seconds, and
the re-crossings arrive 200 to 270 ms after the previous counted
blink, past the 150 ms refractory period's reach. This is the first
independent-face evidence for the residue Eyeblink8 already showed:
after the refractory fix, 72 false alarms remained and 45 sat on top
of real blinks.

WHY THE STATE MACHINE ALLOWS IT
-------------------------------

A closure arms as a blink candidate at 10 percent BELOW the blink
line (fix #114's depth arming), and nothing above the line is
required afterwards: the moment a reopen crosses the line and counts,
the very next dip 10 percent under it arms again. A slow, deep
blinker's lid takes a long time to rise, and while it hovers near the
line it wobbles more than the 10 percent sliver, so one blink mints
several counts. The refractory period cannot cover this without
growing past 200 to 270 ms, which is genuine double-blink territory,
and a timer that eats real double blinks trades one defect for
another.

THE CANDIDATE FIX
-----------------

A re-arm gate, symmetric to the arm line: after a COUNTED blink, no
new closure may arm until the aperture has risen at least 10 percent
ABOVE the blink line. The eyelid must actually reopen before it can
blink again, which is what an eyelid does.

Why this is not the reopen latch that fix #114's review killed: that
design moved the REOPEN line itself, so time spent between two lines
belonged to neither state and durations corrupted. The gate moves
neither line. Closing and reopening stay at the same threshold,
every duration keeps its 4.3 definition, and the gate touches only
whether the NEXT closure may arm.

The cost, stated before the fix rather than discovered after: a lid
that hovers inside the 10 percent band above the line, after a
counted blink, has its subsequent blinks suppressed until it rises
out of the band. That band is a nearly-shut eye on a sound baseline,
territory where PERCLOS and the long-closure detector are the
instruments and a blink count is already dubious, but it is a real
suppression and it is the trade being made.

THE EXPERIMENT, each with its prediction
----------------------------------------

Built on the blink-trace harness, real blinkStep, synthetic aperture
traces, swept across sampling rates 25 to 90 Hz and phases, the same
method that settled the sampling-rate question.

Trace R, the P1 shape: one deep blink with a slow reopen that sags
back to below the arm line 200 to 270 ms after counting, peaking
BETWEEN the blink line and the re-arm line before sagging.
  PREDICTION: current code counts 2 at every rate and phase. With the
  gate, 1 at every rate and phase.

Trace D, a genuine double blink: two full, deep blinks 300 to 400 ms
apart whose lid rises well above the re-arm line in between.
  PREDICTION: both designs count 2, at every rate and phase. If the
  gate ever counts 1 here, the gate eats real double blinks and is
  wrong, whatever else it fixes.

Trace P, the boundary: a genuine double blink whose lid reopens only
into the band between the two lines.
  PREDICTION: current counts 2, the gate counts 1. This is the cost
  above, measured rather than argued.

Durations: PREDICTION: byte-identical between the two designs on
every trace, because the gate touches arming only.

Mutation: PREDICTION: deleting the gate resurrects trace R's double
count and turns its test red, and no other test moves.

THE DECISION RULE, fixed before the run
---------------------------------------

The gate ships if and only if: trace R goes to 1 at every rate and
phase, trace D stays 2 at every rate and phase, and durations are
untouched. If trace D loses blinks at ANY rate, the gate does not
ship and this document records the refutation. Trace P's cost is
accepted in advance and does not block.

If the gate ships, the corpus is re-measured and the numbers
republished, the same discipline as the ruler freeze. The prediction
for that run: total detections FALL (the 72 remaining Eyeblink8
false alarms contain re-crossing doubles), recall moves little
because the refractory fix already proved removing doubles costs no
recall, so precision RISES while recall holds within a point.
