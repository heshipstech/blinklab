THE APERTURE NOISE FLOOR, AND A PREDICTION
==========================================

Written 5 September 2026 (roadmap 10.7a), before any statistic below
is computed, in the predict-then-verify discipline.

WHY IT EXISTS
-------------
Every millimetre the page prints rides apertureMm, and no committed
number says how much that measurement wobbles when nothing happens.
The shallow-blink work talks in 0.4 mm bands, the long-closure line
sits at 40 percent of a baseline, the validation round chased 0.1 mm
disagreements — all against a noise floor nobody has measured. This
row measures it from data already in the repository: the recorded
fixture test/fixtures/session-01.json, 300 frames of the owner's own
face through a real webcam, the same frames the aperture tests already
read at 1280x720 (the fixture's established test convention).

THE METHOD, FIXED NOW
---------------------
The real pipeline, both eyes: core's apertureMm with the committed eye
and iris index sets, at 1280x720. A frame is EXCLUDED when either
eye's aperture reads below 75 percent of that eye's own session
median — the fixture holds two real blinks, and a noise floor polluted
by blink slopes would not be a floor. The excluded count is reported.
No pose gate is applied in this row: the fixture is a straight-face
calibration clip, and the gate joins in row b, where the cross-eye
refusal threshold is DERIVED rather than chosen. Statistics: over
kept frames, the median and 95th percentile of the absolute
frame-to-frame aperture change per eye (a delta counts only when both
of its endpoint frames are kept — a delta bridging an excluded blink
would smuggle the blink back in), and the median and 95th percentile
of the absolute left-minus-right disagreement per frame.

THE PREDICTION
--------------
Committed before the tool runs:

  (i)   per-eye median |frame-to-frame change| at or below 0.15 mm,
  (ii)  per-eye 95th percentile at or below 0.50 mm,
  (iii) cross-eye median |left - right| between 0.2 and 1.5 mm,
  (iv)  the exclusion rule keeps at least 270 of the 300 frames.

Grounds: the two real blinks span a few frames each; a still face's
landmark jitter has always looked small next to the 0.4 mm band the
sampling work discusses; and the eyes of one face genuinely differ,
so the cross-eye number should be well above the temporal one.

WHAT THE NUMBER IS AND IS NOT. This floor is ONE face, ONE camera,
ONE lighting, at the fixture's frame rate. It is the repository's
first committed wobble number, not a property of the instrument in
general; the per-device evaluation matrix (row 11.6) is where a
device-conditioned version would come from. Whatever the numbers say,
they are recorded below and the prediction is scored against them,
not edited.


THE FLOOR, MEASURED, 5 September 2026
=====================================

Computed by the committed pipeline (src/core/apertureNoise.ts) over
the fixture, and recomputed by test/core/apertureNoise.test.ts on
every run, so these numbers cannot drift from the code:

  kept frames: 270 of 300 (30 excluded by the 75 percent rule)
  left median delta mm: 0.020    left p95 delta mm: 0.286
  right median delta mm: 0.019   right p95 delta mm: 0.242
  cross-eye median mm: 0.200     cross-eye p95 mm: 0.860

THE PREDICTION, SCORED. All four hold, two of them exactly at their
committed boundaries. (i) and (ii): the per-eye medians (0.020 and
0.019) sit an order of magnitude under the 0.15 bound, and both p95s
clear 0.50. (iii): the cross-eye median lands at 0.200, on the lower
edge of the predicted 0.2 to 1.5 band — the two eyes agree better
than predicted. (iv): exactly 270 frames kept, the committed minimum
to the frame.

WHAT IT MEANS. On this face, this camera, this light: the aperture's
frame-to-frame wobble at stillness is about 0.02 mm at the median —
temporal landmark noise is NOT what hides shallow blinks, which live
in a band twenty times wider. The tail is fatter: one frame pair in
twenty jumps a quarter millimetre with nothing happening, which is
the number any per-frame threshold must respect. And the two eyes of
this one face disagree by 0.2 mm at the median and 0.86 mm at p95
while both are open and still — ten times the temporal floor — so a
cross-eye disagreement refusal (row 10.7b) must be derived from that
distribution, not from the temporal one, or it would refuse every
frame of an honest face. One face, one camera, one lighting; the
device-conditioned version is row 11.6's to measure.
