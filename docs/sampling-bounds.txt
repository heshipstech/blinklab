SAMPLING-ERROR BOUNDS FOR PERCLOS, AND A PREDICTION
===================================================

Written 5 September 2026 (roadmap 10.10a), before the sweep runs, in
the predict-then-verify discipline.

WHY IT EXISTS
-------------
The page prints PERCLOS with no statement of how wrong the number can
be purely from sampling: a camera at f frames per second reads the eye
at regular instants, and every closure's edges land between two reads.
Blink RATE already has its sampling story committed — the catch
probability tables in docs/blink-sample-rate.txt, swept through the
real detector — so this row does not re-derive rate. It bounds the one
headline number that had no bound, and the on-page "at least ±" lines
(row 10.10b) will quote both: this bound for PERCLOS, the committed
catch tables for rate.

THE METHOD, FIXED NOW
---------------------
A seeded, deterministic sweep (a hand-rolled linear congruential
generator in src/core/samplingBounds.ts — no Math.random, so the
committed table reproduces bit for bit). The generator shape, stated
because the bound inherits it: a 60 second window (the PERCLOS window
the app uses) holding N non-overlapping closures, N drawn 2 to 10,
each duration drawn uniformly between 0.5 and 6 seconds and the set
scaled to hit the target closed fraction P exactly, placed uniformly.
Target fractions P in {0.05, 0.15, 0.30}. Sampling at f in {15, 25,
30, 60} frames per second with a uniform random phase. Per (P, f)
cell: 2000 draws, and the bound is the 95th percentile of the
absolute error between the sampled fraction and the true P. This is
the SAMPLING TERM ONLY: it says nothing about the shut line's
instrument-adjusted placement (amendment 6) or the landmark noise
floor (docs/aperture-noise-floor.txt), and the 10.10b labels must say
so.

THE PREDICTION
--------------
Committed before the sweep runs. Regular sampling of an interval
union is boundary-limited, not binomial: each closure contributes at
most about one sample period of quantization per edge, so the error
should scale with closure COUNT over window length, and inversely
with f — not with sqrt of the sample count, and not much with P
except through N.

  (i)   at 30 fps, every cell's p95 absolute error is at or below
        0.010 of PERCLOS (one percentage point);
  (ii)  at 15 fps, at or below 0.020;
  (iii) halving the frame rate at most doubles a cell's bound
        (within 25 percent slack);
  (iv)  at fixed f, the P=0.30 bound is under twice the P=0.05
        bound — the fraction itself is not the driver.

If the bounds come out this small, the honest page line is not a wide
error bar but a sentence: the sampling term is negligible next to the
instrument's other stated conditions. That is a finding, not a
disappointment. Whatever the numbers say, they are recorded below and
scored against this section, not edited.

THE MECHANISM, VISIBLE IN THE TABLE. Every cell's p95 is exactly two
samples' worth of the window: 2/900 at 15 fps, 2/1500 at 25, 2/1800
at 30, 2/3600 at 60. The error is boundary quantization — each
closure edge can miscount at most about one sample — and the closure
count distribution is the same at every P, which is why the columns
are identical down the fractions. The bound in SAMPLES is the
constant; the bound in fraction is that constant over the sample
count.

THE PREDICTION, SCORED. All four hold, (i) and (ii) by an order of
magnitude: 0.0011 against the 0.010 bound at 30 fps, 0.0022 against
0.020 at 15. (iii): halving the rate doubles the bound to the digit.
(iv): the P=0.30 bound is not merely under twice the P=0.05 bound, it
is identical — the fraction is not the driver at all, the edge count
is.

WHAT IT MEANS. The sampling term of PERCLOS is a rounding error: a
tenth of a percentage point at 30 fps, two tenths at 15. Nobody needs
an error bar for it; they need the sentence row 10.10b will print —
that the number's real conditions are the instrument-adjusted shut
line (amendment 6) and the landmark floor
(docs/aperture-noise-floor.txt), not the camera's frame rate. For
blink RATE the story is the opposite and already committed: the catch
tables in docs/blink-sample-rate.txt show shallow quick blinks LOST
outright at 25-30 fps, so the rate's honest label is "a floor, not a
count" — and 10.10b quotes each number's own document.
