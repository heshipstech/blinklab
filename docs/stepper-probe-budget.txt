THE PROBE BUDGET AT ORDINARY RATES
Roadmap 10.14d. Written 7 September 2026, BEFORE the measurement, as
this repository requires of anything it measures.


WHAT 10.14c LEFT BEHIND

Calibration may spend CALIBRATION_ATTEMPTS seeks, which is 60, to
gather CALIBRATION_FRAMES distinct frames, which is 12. Row 10.14c
replaced a constant 10 ms probe with a quarter of the smallest gap
seen so far, floored at 2 ms, because a constant probe could not
measure a clip above 200 frames per second at all.

A quarter of a period is safe and slow. Three of every four probes
land on the frame already showing, cost an attempt and teach nothing.
That row measured the price at one rate: 30 frames per second went
from 38 attempts to 45 of the 60 available. It recorded the number and
handed the question here rather than pretending the budget was fine.

The budget has to cover more than the arithmetic. Every imprecise
landing, one where the browser answered `seeked` but no frame callback
arrived, also costs an attempt and adds no frame. On a real clip in a
real browser those are not rare. So the question is not only how many
attempts the rule needs on a perfect clip; it is how much room is left
over for the clip that does not answer cleanly.


WHAT IS PREDICTED, AND HOW IT IS SCORED

All predictions are about the fake-video harness in
test/io/videoStepper.test.ts, which records every seek the stepper
asks for, on a clip whose frame times are exact. There are no
imprecise landings in that harness, so what it measures is the
arithmetic floor: the fewest attempts the rule can possibly spend.

P1. Attempts to gather 12 frames are roughly FLAT across rate, not
    rising with it. The rule is proportional to the period it just
    measured, so 30, 60, 120 and 240 frames per second should all cost
    about the same number of probes, within 5 of each other.

P2. That flat cost is between 40 and 50 of the 60 budget at every rate
    from 30 to 300.

P3. The margin left for imprecise landings is therefore under 20
    attempts, or under a third of the budget, at every rate.

P4. Raising the step from a quarter to a half of the smallest gap
    would cost about half as many attempts, and would still measure
    every rate from 30 to 300 correctly ONCE the smallest gap is a
    true single period. It is the early gaps, before that is true,
    where a half is unsafe.

P5. The 60 frames per second end-to-end fixture and the 30 frames per
    second case measure the same count and the same interval after
    whatever change this row makes as before it.


THE DECISION RULE, FIXED NOW

If P1 fails, and attempts rise with rate, then the rule is not
proportional in the way it was designed to be and this row rewrites
the rule rather than the budget.

If P2 and P3 hold, the margin is too thin and something changes. The
row does NOT get to pick the change after seeing the numbers: the
order of preference is fixed here, cheapest and least behavioural
first.

  1. Adapt the step. Once the whole-multiple rule can be run over the
     landings gathered so far and returns a period, use half of THAT
     period rather than a quarter of the smallest raw gap. The
     quarter exists to survive a smallest gap that is up to four
     times the true period; a reduced period is not that, so the
     safety it buys is already bought.
  2. Raise the budget. A seek is cheap in the harness and not free in
     a browser, so this is second, and if it is taken the new number
     is justified by the measured margin rather than rounded up.
  3. Change the floor. Last, because 2 ms is what stops a pathological
     clip driving the step to zero and it is not what costs attempts
     at ordinary rates.

If P5 fails at any point, the change is REVERTED rather than shipped
with a caveat, on the same grounds as 10.14c: a fix for the budget
that moves the behaviour of ordinary clips is not this row.

If P4 fails, option 1 above is off the table and the row takes option
2, because a rule that measures a rate wrongly is not a saving.


WHAT WILL BE COMMITTED EITHER WAY

A table of attempts against rate, measured, in this file. Not a
sentence saying the budget is comfortable. The margin left for
imprecise landings stated as a number of attempts, so a later row that
makes landings less reliable can see what it is spending.
