THE BLINK-DURATION DEVICE ANOMALY: THE ARITHMETIC CANDIDATE FIRST
=================================================================

Written 18 September 2026, before the read it predicts. Roadmap 10.9's
first candidate [C2's arithmetic], committed under the same discipline
as docs/inference-once.txt's arithmetic stage: the prediction and its
falsifier land in their own commit, the read happens on the owner's
retained dry-run exports afterwards, and the outcome is appended below
as a dated section that edits nothing above itself.

THE ANOMALY. Across the dry-run sessions, one person's deliberate
blinks read very different median durations by device: one Mac at
about four processing stamps per delivered frame read 166 ms against
one at about one stamp reading 149 ms, while the two iPhones read 96
and 97 ms at one and two stamps. Two candidate mechanisms are named by
the row, and they are tested in order of cheapness: first the line
geometry (this document, arithmetic on existing columns, no camera),
then the re-read candidate (a fresh paired session on one Mac behind a
60 Hz and a 120 Hz display, because stamps per delivered frame is not
a column the dry-run exports carry), then live cued sessions with the
stepped device-independence control.

THE MECHANISM UNDER TEST. A blink's duration is timed between
crossings of the blink line, and the two placements put that line at
different heights of each device's own swing:

- The PASSIVE line is half the frozen open baseline, measured from
  zero: line = 0.5 x B (BASELINE_THRESHOLD_FRACTION in
  src/core/constants.ts, applied by personalThresholdMm to the p90
  baseline B).
- The GUIDED line (docs/shut-line-rule.txt, blink k = 0.5) is halfway
  up the swing itself: line = C + 0.5 x (B - C), where C is the closed
  baseline the export carries as shutBaselineMm.

Write rho = C/B, each device's closed-to-open ratio. As a fraction f
of the swing above closed:

    f_guided  = 0.5                     on every device, by design
    f_passive = (0.5 - rho) / (1 - rho) which varies with the device

Two immediate consequences, both checkable before any read:

1. The guided line sits above the passive one by exactly the factor
   (1 + rho). The row's own parenthetical — "the guided line sits
   30-50% above the passive one" — is therefore the statement that
   rho runs about 0.3 to 0.5 in these exports. The read should
   confirm that as a sanity check before anything else.
2. At rho -> 0.5 the passive line touches the closed baseline
   (f_passive -> 0) and time-below-line collapses toward zero: the
   same geometry that produced the shallow-blink refusal band. The
   passive placement is EXTREMELY sensitive to rho at the top of the
   band; the guided placement is immune to rho by construction.

THE SHAPE MODEL. As in the committed raised-cosine sweep beside
docs/blink-sample-rate.txt, take a full blink of true duration D with
aperture a(t) = B - (B - C) x (1 - cos(2 pi t / D)) / 2. The fraction
of D spent below a line at swing fraction f is

    T(f) = 1 - arccos(2f - 1) / pi

so the measured duration is T(f) x D, and device ratios cancel D
wherever the true blinks are comparable. Worked values:

    rho    f_passive   T(f_passive)     T(f_guided)
    0.30     0.286        0.359            0.500
    0.35     0.231        0.319            0.500
    0.40     0.167        0.268            0.500
    0.45     0.091        0.194            0.500
    0.50     0.000        0.000            0.500

The read is arithmetic through this model: B re-derived per session as
the p90 of its apertureMm column (the birth formula's own percentile),
C as its shutBaselineMm, rho and f_passive from those, and the
question is whether the geometry column T(f_passive) reproduces the
duration ordering 166 : 149 : 96 : 97. One stated assumption, named
rather than hidden: the dry-run sessions are one person's deliberate
blinks, taken as comparable in true duration across devices. That
assumption is exactly why the row's later stages exist — the cued
protocol gives a denominator and the stepped control removes the
device — so this stage can only rank the candidates, never convict.

THE PREDICTION, committed before the read.

P1. WITHIN THE MACS (166 vs 149 ms, ratio 1.114): the geometry will
    NOT carry it. To carry it, the four-stamp Mac's rho would have to
    sit about 0.05 BELOW the one-stamp Mac's (T moves -11% per +0.05
    of rho in the middle of the band). Predicted: the two Macs' rho
    gap is under 0.05, or in the wrong direction. The grounds are
    13.8a's scored arithmetic: the four-stamp Mac's A/V inflation
    (3.04x the Mac control) is rate-linked, and neither amplitude nor
    velocity reads the line at all, so that evidence sits on an axis
    the line geometry cannot reach. The 17 ms gap is also smaller
    than one 30 fps frame period (33 ms), and the outcome section
    must say so rather than over-read a sub-resolution difference.

P2. MAC VERSUS IPHONE (149 vs 96/97 ms, ratio about 0.64): the
    geometry will carry PART of it, direction committed — the
    iPhones' rho sits HIGHER than the Macs' (their passive line lower
    in their swing, shortening measured durations). For geometry to
    carry the WHOLE gap, the rho spread must reach about 0.10-0.13
    (e.g. Macs at 0.30 needing iPhones at 0.43). Predicted: the
    spread is positive but smaller than that, so re-deriving every
    session at the guided placement (f = 0.5 everywhere) NARROWS the
    Mac-iPhone gap without closing it to within one frame period,
    leaving a platform residue for the re-read candidate and the
    stepped control to allocate.

P3. THE ORDERING TEST that decides between the candidates at this
    stage: under the guided placement the geometry term is constant,
    so whatever duration ordering survives re-derivation is NOT the
    line. Predicted survivor: 4-stamp Mac > 1-stamp Mac at about the
    same ratio (the stamps candidate), Macs > iPhones by a reduced
    but nonzero margin.

THE FALSIFIER, fixed here: if the re-derived durations at the guided
placement CONVERGE across all four sessions to within one frame
period of the slowest device (33 ms at 30 fps), then the anomaly was
the passive line's geometry all along; the camera-side hypothesis is
falsified AT THIS FIRST STAGE, the fresh paired-display session is
not run (there would be nothing left for it to explain), and the next
instrument row is the guided line's adoption cascade (12.0a's own
scoring), not a display-rate session. If instead the rho values land
OUTSIDE the 0.3-0.5 band the row's parenthetical encodes, the
sanity check has failed and the read stops there: the arithmetic was
built on a mischaracterised export and the mischaracterisation is the
finding.

WHAT THIS STAGE CANNOT DO, stated in advance: it cannot convict the
camera. A surviving gap is consistent with the stamps candidate but
proves only that the line does not explain it; conviction needs the
paired session (same Mac, same person, 60 Hz against 120 Hz display,
prediction committed before that read as this row's Check requires)
and acquittal of the devices needs the same clip stepped on each
(15.4a), where disagreement beyond one frame period falsifies the
camera-side hypothesis and names the next instrument row.
