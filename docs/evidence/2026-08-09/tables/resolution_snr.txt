THE PUBLISHED FIGURE, and the rule that produces it.
Shrink each eye box to a quarter of its width and a quarter of its height.
That keeps one pixel in sixteen, so about 94 pixels in every 100 are gone.
Work out the change in each clip's middle blink strength, then average the
eight clips. The answer is +2.7%.

The sign is positive. A smaller picture did not make the blink weaker.
So a picture too small to see the blink is not why blinks are missed.
