ONE PERSONAL RULER: THE SHUT-LINE RULE, WRITTEN FIRST
=====================================================

Written 10 September 2026, before any dry-run column is read for
this question and before the owner's droop session exists, in the
predict-then-verify discipline. Roadmap row 12.0a, brief C2, the
corpus rule at the head of Phase 12.

THE DEFECT THE RULE FIXES
-------------------------
This instrument owns two "eyes shut" consumers — PERCLOS and the
long-closure detector — and both draw their line the same way:
EYES_SHUT_FRACTION (0.4) times shutBaselineMm, where shutBaselineMm
is the aperture baseline frozen the first time the baseline reducer
reads ready (src/core/longClosure.ts, src/core/perclos.ts,
main.ts's frozen copy). That is a POPULATION fraction applied to a
number that was never measured as this person's shut eye: the
frozen baseline is an open-eye statistic, and 40 percent of it is a
guess about where "shut" lives that no committed measurement has
ever checked. Meanwhile the guided calibration already measures the
person's OWN closed median — and only the blink line uses it. One
eye, two rulers, and the shut one borrowed.

THE FORM, ONE FOR BOTH LINES
----------------------------
Every personal line becomes the same expression:

    line = closedMedian + k x (openMedian - closedMedian)

- The BLINK line is the k = 0.5 instance: today's midpoint
  (guidedCalibration.ts places it at (open + closed) / 2), restated
  in the common form with NO behavioural change. The corpus cannot
  move on this restatement, by arithmetic.
- The SHUT line is the new instance, and its k is pre-registered
  here, from the only wobble numbers this repository has committed.

THE SHUT k, DERIVED BEFORE ANY DATA IS READ
-------------------------------------------
The 10.7a noise floor (docs/aperture-noise-floor.txt, recomputed by
test on every run) says: per-eye p95 |frame-to-frame change| 0.286
and 0.242 mm on the fixture face. A shut line's one job is that an
eye resting at its own closed median reads shut frame after frame,
so the line must sit above closedMedian by a margin that p95 tail
cannot cross; and the convention this code already uses for "clear
of a line" is the 10 percent hysteresis band
(APERTURE_HYSTERESIS_FRACTION, blink arm and long-closure re-arm
alike).

    k = 0.15, with the ADOPTION CONDITION that the margin it buys
    is real in millimetres:

    0.15 x (openMedian - closedMedian) >= 0.6 mm

0.6 mm is twice the worst committed per-eye p95 (2 x 0.286 =
0.572, rounded up, the same clear-it-by-a-factor stance the
hysteresis band takes). A guided pair whose span cannot fund the
margin does NOT get a personal shut line: the session keeps the
population rule and the record says which rule ran — the absence
rule, not a silent downgrade. The guided flow's own separation gate
(closed at least 30 percent below open) makes the condition
satisfiable at ordinary spans: any open median of 4 mm or more at
the minimum legal separation funds it.

Two placement invariants, refusals rather than clamps:
- The shut line must sit BELOW the blink line's lower hysteresis
  band: 0.15 x span against 0.45 x span, ordered by construction
  for any positive span; a violation means the inputs are not a
  sound guided pair and the placement refuses.
- k itself never moves per session. A k fitted to a session is not
  a rule, it is the session grading itself.

ADOPTION: the personal shut line is used by PERCLOS and the
long-closure detector exactly when a guided line exists for the
session (the same condition under which the blink line goes
personal), lifting their baseline-refusal the way it lifts the
blink line's. No guided line, no change: the population rule
stands, and a clip never has a stored line by construction.

THE PREDICTIONS
---------------
P1 (floor shift, testable from the dry-run exports' existing
    shutBaselineMm and apertureMm columns, with proxies fixed now:
    openMedian proxied by the session's median apertureMm over
    trusted rows, closedMedian by its 1st percentile). On sessions
    whose frozen baseline was sound — near the open-aperture
    median — the personal shut line lands BELOW 0.4 x
    shutBaselineMm: 0.4 of an open-eye number sits near mid-lid,
    while closed + 0.15 x span hugs the lid. Time-below-line can
    only fall or hold there.
P2 (the P80 phone trace, named by the row's own Check). Where the
    frozen baseline froze wrong-low, 0.4 x baseline can sit at or
    under the noise around closed and count nothing. Anchored at
    the person's own closed value plus a funded margin, the
    personal line sits ABOVE such a line, and the P80 dry-run
    phone trace reads NONZERO PERCLOS under its own floor.
P3 (the droop session, the owner's, protocol below). A sustained
    deliberate droop reads as ONE long-closure event under the
    personal line, and the two placements disagree on duration in
    the direction of the personal line starting the event earlier
    or refusing it — whichever way, the pair of numbers is
    published side by side.
P4 (Eyeblink8). The stepped corpus is unchanged by construction:
    no stored guided line exists on a clip, so neither consumer's
    line moves there. Scored digit-for-digit on the owner's next
    corpus run.

THE FALSIFIERS, NAMED BEFORE THE NUMBERS
----------------------------------------
- If the P80 trace still reads zero PERCLOS under its own floor,
  the premise that its zero belonged to the LINE rather than the
  eyes is falsified, and the outcome section says so; the rule does
  not gain an exception to rescue it.
- If, on the sound dry-run sessions, the adoption condition fails
  more often than it holds, k = 0.15 is too timid to matter at
  real spans; the outcome records that verdict and any change to k
  is a NEW dated section with its own derivation, never an edit
  here.
- If either placement invariant fires on a session the guided flow
  accepted, the guided gate and this rule disagree about what a
  sound pair is, and the disagreement is the finding.

THE DROOP SESSION (owner, one recording)
----------------------------------------
One session on the usual laptop setup, exported and kept local as
always: two minutes ordinary sitting, then on a cue three slow
deliberate droops — eyes allowed to fall heavy and hover nearly
shut for five to ten seconds each, without fully closing where
possible — separated by normal open stretches. The session is run
through BOTH placements (the retained CSV holds every column both
need; the instrument is analysis/blinklab/shutline.py, one command
in tools/shutline_compare.py), and the published outcome is the
pair of eyes-shut shares and the pair of longest-below-line runs —
the runs never summed, because adding them would be the dilution
defect wearing a new column — side by side, in a dated section
below this one. Row 12.0a ticks on that section — not on this
rule.
