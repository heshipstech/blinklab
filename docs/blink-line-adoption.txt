ADOPTING THE GUIDED BLINK LINE, AND A PREDICTION
================================================

Written 2 September 2026, before the change is made and before any
new measurement is read, in the predict-then-verify discipline.

WHAT THE CHANGE WOULD BE
------------------------
The detector's blink line is today HALF of each person's own learned
open aperture (src/core/baseline.ts): the passive baseline learns the
open eye over thirty seconds and ASSUMES the closed eye is near zero,
so it places the line at half of open. The guided calibration built
over increments 2a-2d measures a person's real open AND real closed
aperture through two held phases and places their line at the true
MIDPOINT of the two (src/core/guidedCalibration.ts). It is stored per
device (blinklab-blink-calibration-v1) and, today, feeds nothing.

The proposed change: when a stored guided line exists, the detector
uses it in place of the passive half-of-open line; when none exists,
nothing changes. The selection is the whole change — "prefer the
person's own measured line, fall back to the baseline" — and it is
meant to be a small, pure, testable decision.

WHAT IS ALREADY KNOWN, AND WHY THE PREDICTION IS DULL
-----------------------------------------------------
The awake corpus autopsy (docs/miss-character.txt) and the iris result
(docs/iris-occlusion.txt) already classified all 67 Eyeblink8 misses,
and NONE of them is a threshold-placement failure:

  ~49  a recall CEILING. On the frames a human marked shut, neither the
       aperture nor the iris drops (in-span iris median 0.846 vs 0.877
       open, 0/49 below 0.70). The closure is invisible to the model's
       landmarks. No line, however placed, catches a closure the
       aperture does not register: the aperture never dips below ANY
       line on these misses.
    9  long closures rejected by MAX_BLINK_DURATION_MS = 500. A duration
       cap, not a threshold; a different line does not change it.
    5  re-arm / refractory state-machine drops. A state-machine
       parameter, not a threshold.
    4  no trusted face at all. No measurement to threshold.

A better-placed line moves recall only by catching a real aperture dip
that the old line sat above. The autopsy found no such miss: the misses
are ceiling, duration, state-machine and no-face. So a differently
placed line has nothing in this corpus to catch.

There is a second reason the guided line cannot rescue the ceiling
misses even in principle. resolveGuidedCalibration REFUSES when the
closed median is not at least 30% below the open one
(GUIDED_CALIBRATION_MIN_SEPARATION_FRACTION) - which is exactly the
ceiling condition, a closure the instrument barely registers. So on the
very population where the ceiling bites, a guided run produces no line
to adopt: it refuses, and the detector falls back to the baseline. The
guided line is designed to decline precisely where a threshold move
would be a guess.

And for a TYPICAL subject, whose closed eye really does read near zero,
the guided midpoint (open + closed)/2 is approximately half of open -
the passive line's own value. Guided and passive nearly coincide where
closed is near zero; they diverge only for ATYPICAL eyelids, whose
closed aperture sits well above zero.

THE PREDICTION
--------------
Scored on a corpus run in which each clip carries a guided line derived
from its own open and closed aperture (the honest offline analog of a
person calibrating; the ceiling clips' own refusal is part of the test),
compared against the committed baseline run:

  Eyeblink8 recall and precision change by essentially nothing - within
  the run-to-run noise of the benchmark, no category of the 67 misses
  recovered, no new false positive introduced. The guided line has
  nothing to catch that the passive line missed, and on the ceiling
  clips it refuses and falls back.

Equivalently, and this is the part that ships now: with NO stored
calibration - which is every corpus run as the runner exists today,
using a fresh browser with empty storage - the change is neutral BY
CONSTRUCTION. loadBlinkCalibration returns null, the selection returns
the baseline line, and the detector is byte-for-byte what it was. The
committed Eyeblink8 numbers do not move because the path that reads the
guided line is not reached in the corpus.

WHERE THE VALUE ACTUALLY IS, AND WHAT THIS DOES NOT CLAIM
---------------------------------------------------------
The guided line is not a corpus-recall improvement and this change does
not claim to be one. Its value is PERSONALISATION for atypical eyelids,
where the passive half-of-open assumption is simply wrong. The one
recorded case is the owner's own: naturally low eyelids that "sat below
the blink line for five seconds while fully awake" (ROADMAP acceptance
note 5, the reason the long-closure detector moved to a dedicated shut
line). For such a person the passive line is mis-placed, and a line
measured from their real open and closed aperture should place it right.

That benefit is a HYPOTHESIS grounded in one observation, not a
verified result. Eyeblink8 cannot test it: its subjects are
pre-recorded and cannot perform a two-phase guided calibration, and it
was not selected for atypical eyelids. So the change ships as an
opt-in personalisation whose default (no calibration) is exactly
today's behaviour, and it is described that way - "set your own blink
line", not "improve accuracy". Whether it helps a real atypical user is
the next thing to actually measure, and it needs a calibrated subject
with labelled blinks, not this corpus.

WHAT WOULD REFUTE THIS
----------------------
  If a corpus run with per-clip guided lines moved recall
  significantly, the autopsy's "the misses are not threshold-placement"
  conclusion was wrong, and the line - not the model ceiling - would be
  back in question.

  If such a run moved PRECISION significantly (new false positives from
  a higher guided line arming on ordinary droops), the guided midpoint
  is too generous and the selection or the separation floor needs
  reconsidering before it is offered as a default anywhere.

  If, with no stored calibration, any committed Eyeblink8 number
  changed at all, the "neutral by construction" claim is false and the
  selection is reaching the guided path when it must not.

This prediction is not edited after the fact; the outcome becomes a new
dated section below it.
